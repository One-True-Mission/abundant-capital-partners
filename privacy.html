Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Abundant Capital Partners ("we," "us," or "our") collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can do about it. We collect information about investment transactions, so we have written this to be specific rather than generic.

Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to submit through our forms or provide to us directly. Depending on which form you use, that may include:

  • Your first and last name, email address, and phone number
  • The address of a property you are financing or considering
  • Transaction details: whether you are under contract, your closing deadline, purchase price, after repair value, rehab budget, requested loan amount, and exit strategy
  • The name of the entity you intend to borrow in
  • The number of borrowers or guarantors on the transaction
  • Ranges describing your investing experience, approximate credit score, and available liquidity
  • Any message or notes you write to us
  • Whether you consented to being contacted by phone, text message, and email

What we deliberately do not collect here

We do not collect Social Security numbers, dates of birth, government identification numbers, bank account or routing numbers, or uploaded financial documents through this website. Credit, liquidity, and experience are collected as ranges rather than exact figures, because ranges are all we need to evaluate initial fit.

If a transaction proceeds and underwriting requires verification documents or additional detail, we will collect that information through a secure channel, and its handling will be described to you at that time. We will never ask you to send sensitive information by unencrypted email.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the time of your visit, for security and operational purposes. This website does not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, session recording, or keystroke logging, and we do not capture form entries before you press a button to submit them.

Two-step forms

Our deal submission form has two steps. When you press "Continue to Deal Details," the contact information from step one is submitted to us at that point, before you complete step two. This is deliberate and it is disclosed on the form itself: it means that if you stop partway through, we still have enough to follow up with you.

Nothing is transmitted before you press a button. If you close the page without pressing anything, nothing reaches us.

How we use your information

  • To evaluate whether and how we can finance your transaction
  • To respond to your questions and communicate with you about your inquiry
  • To determine which loan programs fit your situation
  • To administer, service, and enforce any loan we make
  • To comply with applicable law and our legal obligations
  • To detect and prevent fraud and to protect the security of our systems

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal effects without human involvement.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to do the work you asked us to do, or where the law requires it:

  • Capital sources and lending partners. To structure and place your financing, we may share transaction and borrower information with the capital partners considering your file.
  • Service providers. Parties who help us operate, such as our form processor (Formspree), our website host, our email provider, and the online platform used for construction draw requests.
  • Transaction participants. Where relevant to closing your loan, parties such as title companies, closing attorneys, appraisers or valuation providers, and insurance agents.
  • Professional advisors. Our attorneys, accountants, and auditors.
  • Legal and safety. Where required by law, subpoena, or regulation, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or those of others.
  • Business transfer. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to the protections in this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterward, and we keep records relating to loans we make for as long as required by applicable law and our own recordkeeping obligations, which may be several years after the loan is repaid.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we hold. This website is served over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct information that is inaccurate
  • Request that we delete information, subject to records we are required to retain
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing communications
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP to any message from us

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, and the right to opt out of certain processing. We will honor verified requests to the extent applicable law requires. To make a request, use the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Communications from us

If you provide your phone number and consent, we may contact you by phone, text message, and email about your inquiry. See our Messaging Terms for details, including how to opt out. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Children

This website is directed to real estate investors and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third party sites

This website may link to third party sites and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be reflected on this page, so it is worth checking back if you have an ongoing relationship with us.
