Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Abundant Capital Partners wants this website to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology. Financing information should not be locked behind a design choice.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. That is the standard commonly referenced in the United States for public-facing websites.

What we have done

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure on every page
  • Descriptive alternative text on content images
  • Keyboard access to navigation, buttons, form fields, and the accordion sections
  • Visible focus styling on interactive elements
  • Form labels tied to their inputs, with errors shown as text next to the field rather than by color alone
  • Text contrast checked against the WCAG AA thresholds
  • Support for the operating system's reduced motion setting, which stops the scrolling banner and the automatic image carousel
  • A responsive layout that works down to small phone screens without horizontal scrolling

Where we know there is room to improve

Accessibility is ongoing rather than finished. Areas we continue to work on include the image carousel, which is a pattern that is difficult to make ideal for every assistive technology, and any third party tools or platforms we link to, which we do not control.

If you hit a barrier

Please tell us. If any part of this website prevents you from getting the information you need, contact us and we will provide that information another way, by phone or in a format that works for you. We will also work to fix the underlying issue.

  • Phone: (704) 412-4192
  • Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern. Sunday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern. Closed Saturday.

When you contact us, it helps if you can tell us the page you were on, what you were trying to do, and what assistive technology you were using. None of that is required, and we would rather hear from you without the detail than not hear from you at all.

Third party content

Some content or functionality may be provided by third parties, including the online platform used for construction draw requests. We do not control the accessibility of third party services, but tell us if one creates a barrier and we will help you get what you need directly.
