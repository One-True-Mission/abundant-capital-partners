The Question Nobody Asks Out Loud

Can You Actually Close?

It is the real question behind every call we take, and most investors are too polite to ask it. Here is our answer, before you have to.

Almost every experienced investor has been burned. A lender issues a term sheet, collects a deposit, orders reports, and then goes quiet with days left on the contract. The deal dies. The earnest money is gone. The relationship with that seller is gone too, and so is the next deal that seller would have brought you.

We built this business around not being that lender. We underwrite the hard parts early instead of saving them for the end. If something in a file is going to be a problem, you hear about it in the first conversation, not the last one. And when we say yes, we treat that as an obligation rather than an option.

We don't issue term sheets casually. When we commit to a deal, we're committed to getting it to the closing table.

Aida Kassa, Abundant Capital Partners

The hard questions come first

Title issues, entity structure, contractor gaps, thin liquidity. We surface the things that kill deals at the beginning, while there is still time to solve them.

A term sheet means something here

We do not issue terms to hold your attention while we go looking for capital. If you have terms from us, we have already decided we want the deal.

Multiple capital sources, one point of contact

We work with a range of capital partners, so a file that does not fit one box gets restructured instead of declined. You never have to start over somewhere else.

You get the truth, not the comfortable version

If your deal does not work, we will tell you why and what would have to change. A fast honest no is worth more to you than a slow maybe.

How It Works

Six Steps From Conversation to Capital

No mystery, no moving goalposts. Here is exactly what happens between the first call and the wire.

1

Tell us about your deal

Every deal is unique. We take the time to understand your project, goals, and timeline, identify potential obstacles, and determine the best financing strategy.

2

Explore financing options

We review your scenario and present solutions tailored to your project, including the ones you did not know were available to you.

Who We Work With

First Deal or Fiftieth

We work with everyone from first-time investors completing their first project to experienced operators managing large portfolios.

Your first project

Most hard money lenders require prior deals and will tell you so on the first call. We do not work that way. While we enjoy working with seasoned investors, we especially enjoy helping newer investors successfully complete their first deal.

Expect more explanation, more questions asked of you, and a lender who will point out the thing you did not know to look for. Your first deal sets the pattern for the next twenty. It is worth getting right.

Experienced operators

If you are running multiple projects, a growing rental portfolio, or a value-add multifamily plan, you do not need to be walked through the basics. You need a lender who keeps pace, structures around the plan you already have, and does not restart underwriting every time you bring a new file.

One point of contact, multiple capital sources behind it, and programs that scale from a single door to an entire portfolio.

Who we are not the right fit for

We focus exclusively on investment real estate financing. We are not the right fit for owner-occupied residential mortgages or consumer lending, and we would rather tell you that in the first two minutes than waste a week of your time. If that is what you need, we are glad to point you toward someone who does it well.

Creative Financing

We Solve Financing Problems Other Lenders Cannot

We work with various capital sources, which lets us structure financing solutions for a wide range of investment scenarios. Rather than forcing borrowers into a single lending box, we focus on finding the right execution for each transaction.

Our approach combines speed, creativity, high leverage options, and access to multiple lending programs to maximize approvals without sacrificing execution. In practice that means a file another lender would decline outright often just needs to be structured differently.

  • Bank financing that collapsed close to the closing date
  • A property type that does not fit a standard lending box
  • Income that is real but difficult to document conventionally
  • A timeline shorter than a conventional lender can work with
  • Multiple properties an investor would rather finance as one position
