Who We Are

Private Capital for People Who Buy Property

Abundant Capital Partners is a private lender making business purpose loans secured by non-owner-occupied investment real estate. Every loan we make goes to an investor, for a property held as an investment. That focus is deliberate and it shapes everything about how we work.

Behind the company is more than 20 years in real estate and involvement in funding over 1,000 deals. That experience is not a line on a page. It is the reason we can look at a scenario and recognize it, tell you within a conversation whether it works, and know which of ten programs is the right structure instead of pushing you into whichever one we happen to have.

Our goal is not to become the biggest hard money lender out there. It is to be the first lender investors think of when they need certainty, honest guidance, and creative financing solutions.

We are based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and we work with investors across the country. Investment property lending is not a walk-in business, so where you are matters far less than what the deal looks like. Most of it happens by phone, email, and the title company, whether your property is a mile from our desk or three time zones away.

How We Are Structured

Multiple Capital Sources, One Point of Contact

We work with various capital sources, allowing us to structure financing solutions for a wide range of investment scenarios. Rather than forcing borrowers into a single lending box, we focus on finding the right execution for each transaction.

In practice, this is the single biggest difference between us and a lender running one product. When a file does not fit, a one-box lender declines it. We restructure it. You never have to start the process over somewhere else, re-explain your deal to a stranger, or lose a week you did not have.

  • Ten loan programs across residential, multifamily, and commercial
  • Financing options at institutional level, delivered with personal guidance
  • High leverage options where the deal supports them
  • Speed and creativity applied without sacrificing execution
Who We Work With

First Deal or Fiftieth

We work with everyone from first-time investors completing their first project to experienced operators managing large portfolios.

Your first project

Most hard money lenders require prior deals and will say so on the first call. We do not work that way. While we enjoy working with seasoned investors, we especially enjoy helping newer investors successfully complete their first deal.

Expect more explanation, more questions asked of you, and a lender who points out the thing you did not know to look for. Your first deal sets the pattern for the next twenty, and it is worth getting right rather than getting fast.

Experienced operators

If you are running several projects at once, a growing rental portfolio, or a value-add multifamily plan, you do not need the basics explained. You need a lender who keeps pace, structures around the plan you already have, and does not restart from zero every time you bring a new file.

One point of contact, multiple capital sources behind it, and programs that scale from a single door to an entire portfolio.

Who we are not the right fit for

We focus exclusively on investment real estate financing. We are not the right fit for owner-occupied residential mortgages or consumer lending, and we would rather tell you that in the first two minutes than waste a week of your time. If that is what you need, we are glad to point you toward someone who does it well.

The Kinds of Problems We Solve

Deal Scenarios

These describe categories of situation we are built to handle. They are illustrative of what we do rather than descriptions of specific past transactions.

Financing Collapsed

The bank pulled out two weeks before closing

You were approved, the file went quiet, and now a lender is backing away with the contract about to expire and your earnest money on the line.

What we look at: whether the asset supports a bridge that closes on the original timeline, and what the permanent financing exit looks like once the dust settles.

Draws

You need the next draw released this week

The crew is on site, the work is done, and the money is sitting in a queue at a lender who treats draw requests as a monthly batch job.

What we do: unlimited draws across the project, submitted online, typically released within 24 hours after approval.

Portfolio

Several properties, several separate loans

You own multiple rentals, each with its own note, servicer, and maturity date, and the administrative weight is starting to slow down your acquisitions.

What we look at: consolidating stabilized properties into a single portfolio loan with one closing and one payment.

Unusual Asset

A property type nobody will underwrite

A furnished mid term rental, a mixed use building, a short term rental with real revenue that keeps getting compared to a long term lease that does not reflect it.

What we look at: which of our capital sources has a real box for the asset, rather than forcing it into the wrong one.

Documentation

Income that is real but hard to document

You are self-employed, you write off aggressively, and a conventional lender keeps asking for paperwork that does not reflect what you actually earn.

What we look at: whether the property itself carries the debt, which is the entire premise of a DSCR structure.

First Deal

Your first project, and you are not sure what to ask

You found a property, the numbers look right to you, and you do not yet know which questions separate a good deal from an expensive lesson.

What we do: walk the deal with you, name the risks plainly, and tell you honestly if it is one to pass on.

Real case studies are coming as clients clear them for publication. We would rather show you nothing than show you something invented.
