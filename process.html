From First Call to Funding

What Actually Happens, Step by Step

No mystery, no moving goalposts, and no surprises in the last week. Here is the whole path from the first conversation to the wire, plus straight answers to what investors ask us most.

The Process

Six Steps From Conversation to Capital

Every deal is unique, but the path through is the same one every time. You will always know which step you are on.

1

Tell us about your deal

Every deal is unique. We take the time to understand your project, goals, and timeline in order to understand your objectives, identify potential obstacles, and determine the best financing strategy.

This is also where the hard questions come out. Title, entity structure, who is doing the work, what happens if the timeline slips. We would rather find the problem now, when there is still time to solve it.

2

Explore financing options

We review your scenario and present solutions tailored to your project. Because we work with multiple capital sources rather than one lending box, that often includes a structure you did not know was available to you.

3

Submit documents

We keep the documentation process simple and efficient. By focusing only on the information needed to evaluate your deal, we help reduce unnecessary paperwork and keep your deal moving forward.

You will not get a generic checklist built for the hardest possible file. You will get a list built for yours.

4

Property review and underwriting

We analyze the property, deal structure, and borrower profile while completing the necessary due diligence to move your loan toward approval. This is where valuation, title, and the details of your plan get examined properly.

5

Clear to close

Finalize terms, complete remaining due diligence, and prepare for closing with the title company and everyone else on the file. Nobody should be chasing a missing item on the morning of closing.

6

Funding

Your loan closes and capital is delivered so you can execute your plan. On construction and rehab loans, the draw process starts here and runs for the life of the project.

Setting Expectations

What We Ask For, and What We Never Will

You should know exactly what a lender is going to want from you before you start, and exactly where the line is.

What we ask for up front
  • The property address and how to reach you
  • Whether you are under contract and your closing deadline
  • Purchase price, after repair value, and rehab budget
  • The loan amount you are looking for
  • Your exit strategy: flip and sell, refinance and hold, or sell as-is
  • The entity you intend to borrow in
  • Roughly how many deals you have completed
  • Approximate ranges for credit score and available liquidity
What we never ask for through this website
  • Your Social Security number
  • Your date of birth
  • Bank account or routing numbers
  • Uploaded pay stubs, statements, or tax returns
  • Exact credit scores or exact account balances

Credit, liquidity, and experience are collected as ranges because ranges are all we need to triage a deal. If underwriting later requires verification documents, those move through a secure channel, never a web form and never plain email. Any message asking you to send sensitive documents by email is not coming from us.

Construction and Rehab Loans

How the Draw Process Works

Unlimited construction draws are available throughout the project. You submit a draw request through an online platform as work is completed, the request is reviewed promptly, and funds are typically released within 24 hours after approval.

Ask any experienced flipper what actually costs them money and very few will name the interest rate. They will name the week their crew stood idle waiting on a draw sitting in a queue somewhere. Draw speed is not a convenience feature. On a project with a carrying cost, it is one of the largest real numbers in the whole deal.
