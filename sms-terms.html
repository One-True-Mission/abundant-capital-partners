Messaging Terms

Last updated: July 29, 2026

These terms describe how Abundant Capital Partners ("we," "us," or "our") communicates with you by phone, text message, and email, and how you can stop those communications at any time.

Consent

When you check the consent box on one of our forms and submit your phone number, you agree that we may contact you by phone call, text message (SMS and MMS), and email regarding your inquiry, your transaction, and related matters. Messages may be sent using automated technology.

Your consent is not a condition of obtaining any loan or service from us. You can decline to consent and still speak with us by calling (704) 412-4192.

By consenting, you confirm that you are the subscriber or the customary user of the phone number you provided, and that you are authorized to consent for that number. Please let us know if your number changes or is reassigned.

What we send

Messages relate to your inquiry and any transaction in progress. Typical examples include a reply to a form you submitted, questions about your deal, a request for missing information, scheduling a call, and status updates as your loan moves through underwriting and closing.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your inquiry and whether you have an active transaction with us. There is no fixed schedule and we do not send recurring marketing message campaigns.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Those charges come from your mobile carrier, not from us. Check your plan if you are unsure what your carrier charges.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text message from us and we will stop sending text messages to that number. You may receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed.

You can also opt out by calling us at (704) 412-4192 and asking, or by replying to any email from us and asking us to stop.

If you opt out of text messages, we may still need to contact you by phone or email about an active transaction. If you want to stop all contact, tell us and we will honor that.

How to opt back in

Reply START to resume text messages, or call and ask us to re-enable them.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or call (704) 412-4192 during our hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern, and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern. We are closed Saturday.

Carriers

Supported carriers may change over time, and wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We cannot guarantee that every message will be delivered.

Never send sensitive information by text or email

Do not send Social Security numbers, dates of birth, bank account or routing numbers, or images of financial documents by text message or unencrypted email. We will never ask you to. If you receive a message claiming to be from us that requests that information, do not respond. Call us directly at the number above to verify.

Privacy

Information you send us by message is handled according to our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your phone number and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Changes

We may update these terms by posting a revised version with a new "Last updated" date.
