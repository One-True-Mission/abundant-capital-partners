Disclosures

Last updated: July 29, 2026

These disclosures apply to this website and to all lending activity conducted by Abundant Capital Partners ("Abundant Capital Partners," "we," "us," or "our"). Please read them carefully. They describe important limits on what we do and on what anything published here means.

Business purpose lending only

Abundant Capital Partners makes loans for business purposes only. Every loan we make is secured by non-owner-occupied real property held for investment or business use, and is made to a borrower for a business purpose.

Our loans are not made for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not originate consumer mortgage loans, owner-occupied residential mortgage loans, home equity loans for personal use, or any other form of consumer credit.

If you are seeking financing for a property that you or a member of your family occupy or intend to occupy as a primary or secondary residence, we are not the right lender for you. Tell us and we will try to point you toward someone who handles that type of financing.

Not a commitment to lend

Nothing on this website is an offer to lend, a commitment to lend, or an offer to enter into any loan agreement. Information published here, including descriptions of our loan programs, is provided for general informational purposes and is subject to change at any time without notice.

All loans are subject to underwriting, credit review, property review, valuation, title review, and final approval. Submitting information through this website, speaking with us, or receiving a quote, scenario, or term sheet does not obligate either party and does not guarantee that any loan will be approved or funded.

Terms and program availability

We do not publish rates, points, loan-to-value figures, loan amount ranges, or fees on this website. Terms on investment property financing vary based on the specific structure of each transaction, including the property, the business plan, the timeline, the exit strategy, and the borrower's experience and financial profile.

Any terms discussed with you are specific to the transaction under review at the time they are given, and are subject to change based on the results of underwriting and due diligence. Program availability varies and may be modified or withdrawn at any time.

Where we lend

We work with real estate investors throughout the United States. Program availability varies by state, by property type, and by transaction. Not every program described on this website is available in every state, and some states are subject to additional requirements or restrictions that may affect whether we can fund a particular loan.

References on this website to lending nationwide describe the geographic scope of the investors and properties we consider. They are not a representation that any specific program is available in your state, or that we are able to fund every transaction in every jurisdiction. Tell us where the property is located early in the conversation and we will tell you what is available there.

Capital sources

We work with a range of capital sources in order to structure financing across a variety of investment scenarios. Depending on the transaction, we may act as a lender, arrange financing through one or more capital partners, or both. The structure applicable to your transaction will be identified to you in connection with that transaction.

No legal, tax, investment, or accounting advice

Nothing on this website, and nothing said to you by our personnel, constitutes legal, tax, accounting, appraisal, or investment advice. We are not your attorney, accountant, financial advisor, or fiduciary.

Real estate investing involves risk, including the risk of loss. Content published here, including our educational articles, is general in nature, may not apply to your circumstances, and should not be relied upon as a substitute for advice from qualified professionals of your own choosing.

Illustrative content

Any deal scenarios, examples, or descriptions of situations published on this website are illustrative. They describe categories of circumstances we are equipped to handle. They are not descriptions of specific past transactions, they do not contain actual transaction figures, and they are not a representation, guarantee, or prediction that any particular outcome will be achieved in your transaction.

Statistics we publish about our experience, including years in real estate and number of deals funded, describe cumulative professional experience and are not a guarantee of results on any future transaction.
