What this form will never ask you for

Credit, liquidity, and experience are collected as ranges, because ranges are all we need to point you at the right program. We do not collect the following through this website:

  • Social Security number
  • Date of birth
  • Bank account or routing numbers
  • Uploaded statements, pay stubs, or tax returns

If underwriting later needs verification documents, those move through a secure channel. Any message claiming to be from us and asking you to email sensitive documents is not from us.
